Privacy

Last changed 3 November 2026

Cloud Slicer is a free, open-source 3D printing slicer that runs in your browser. This page says what it keeps about you and why. In short: only what it needs to work, no tracking, no ads, nothing sold.

Without signing in

Everything stays in your browser: your models, settings and projects. Saving a project downloads the file to your device. Slicing runs on your device too, so your models are not sent to us.

When you sign in with Google

Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture. We keep:

  • Your account: name and email, so you can sign in on any device.
  • Your presets and settings: printer, filament and process presets you save, and choices like the settings level.
  • Your printers: their names and addresses, and API keys, which are stored encrypted.
  • A list of your projects: name, size, when it was saved, which device is editing it, a small picture of it and its print history.

Your projects in Google Drive

When you sign in, Google asks if Cloud Slicer may use Google Drive. Cloud Slicer asks only for the permission to see and change the files it creates. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.

  • Your browser saves each project as a 3MF file in a “Cloud Slicer” folder in your Drive. The file goes straight from your browser to Google, not through our servers.
  • Our server uses the permission only to do what you ask: open a project, check that a save arrived, rename it or delete it, and to delete the older copy when you save a newer one.
  • The files are yours. They stay in your Drive if you stop using Cloud Slicer or delete your account.
  • You can say no to Drive. You can still sign in and keep your presets and printers, but projects then save as downloads only. You can connect Drive later from the account menu.
  • You can take the permission away at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Cloud Slicer’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We don’t use it for ads, don’t sell it, and don’t let people read it.

The Device tab

To reach your printers from anywhere, a small bridge program you install connects to our relay. Printer status, camera pictures and files pass through it on their way to you. We keep only what the features need, such as which bridges belong to your account and your alert settings.

What we don’t do

  • No analytics, no tracking cookies, no ads.
  • No automatic error reports. When something goes wrong, the app shows the error with a Copy button, and you decide whether to send it to anyone.
  • We don’t sell your information or give it to anyone.
  • The site runs on Cloudflare, so your account, presets and project list are kept on Cloudflare’s servers.

Deleting your data

The account menu has Delete account. It removes your account, presets, printers, project list and project pictures. Files in your Google Drive stay there, since they are yours; delete the “Cloud Slicer” folder in Drive if you want them gone.

Questions

Cloud Slicer is open source. Ask or report anything on GitHub.
